About Evercrop

We’re building Evercrop to help Singapore’s vegetable growers sell directly to the people who eat what they grow.

For growers

Farm teams can share what’s in stock and use WhatsApp to handle routine questions about prices, packs and collection.

For shoppers

See who grew your vegetables, what’s available and how much each pack costs. Find a simple way to cook something you haven’t tried.
