ABOUT EVERCROP

Making local veg the easy choice.

Evercrop puts Singapore’s farms in your WhatsApp — fresher veg for you, a fairer deal for growers.

of Singapore’s food is imported

WHY WE EXIST

A shorter path from farm to table.

Middlemen add cost and time. We help farms sell straight to the people who eat what they grow — through the app everyone already uses.

One platform, two sides

For shoppers

  • Farm-direct prices
  • Order by chatting on WhatsApp
  • Grow a tree, earn a free harvest

For farms

  • AI assistant answers 24/7
  • Chats turned into clean orders
  • Sell direct, keep the margin
