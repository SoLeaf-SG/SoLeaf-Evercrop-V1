ABOUT EVERCROP

Making local veg the easy choice.

Singapore imports over 90% of its food. Local farms grow great produce, but reaching shoppers directly is hard. Evercrop puts the farm in your WhatsApp.

For shoppers

Know who grew your veg, pay farm-direct prices, and order with a simple chat. Every order grows your tree towards a free harvest pack.

For farms

An AI assistant answers stock and price questions on WhatsApp, collects orders and hands your team a clean summary — so you can sell direct and keep the margin.
