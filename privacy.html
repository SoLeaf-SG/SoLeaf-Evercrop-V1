SoLeaf ("we", "us") runs EverCrop. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain language.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, password (stored securely, never in plain text), and farm name for farms.
  • Orders: items, amounts, delivery address and payment status.
  • Chat messages: what you send to a farm's EverCrop chatbot on WhatsApp, and your WhatsApp number.
  • Rewards: water earned, check-ins and referrals.
  • Usage: basic device and log data to keep the service working.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, process orders and send order updates.
  • To run the tree rewards programme.
  • To improve EverCrop and keep it secure.
  • To send harvest alerts or news, only if you opt in. You can opt out at any time.

We never sell your personal data.

3. WhatsApp and AI

Chats run on WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta under its own privacy policy. Our AI chatbot reads your messages to answer questions and build your order. Messages are used only for that purpose and to improve order accuracy, never to advertise to you.

4. Who we share with

  • The farm you order from: your name, contact, order and delivery details.
  • Service providers: hosting, messaging, payment and AI providers that help us run EverCrop, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

Farms only see their own customers. One farm can't see another farm's data.

5. Storage and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. We use encryption, access controls and regular reviews to protect it. Some providers may store data outside Singapore, and when they do we make sure it gets comparable protection.

6. Your rights

  • Ask to see or correct the personal data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent, for example to marketing, at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to our Data Protection Officer at soleaf.co@gmail.com or use our contact page. We aim to reply within 30 days.
